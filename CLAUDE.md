# MolaMola site rules

- **Drink illustrations are locked.** Read `docs/drink-illustration-system.md` before touching any
  cup, drink, topping or product illustration. Do not redesign them; only refine sticker
  placement, line weight, ingredient proportions and topping accuracy. New drinks are added as
  recipes in `content/drinks.ts`, never as one-off art.
- Content (prices, menus, FAQ, gallery) lives in `content/`. Don't hard-code it in components.
